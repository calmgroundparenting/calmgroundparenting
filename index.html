Questions

Frequently asked

What format is the guide?

A 101-page A4 PDF. It works on phones, tablets and computers, and the worksheets print cleanly at home.

Does it apply outside the US or UK?

Yes. It is written for families anywhere, with practical questions to ask local services wherever you live, rather than one country's systems.

My child is a teenager. Is it still useful?

Yes. The regulation, routine and relationship chapters apply at any age, and Module 5 covers the teenage years, legal adulthood and planning for adult life.

Do I have to finish it in 30 days?

No. The 30-day structure is a suggested pace. If you only have 15 minutes, the guide tells you exactly which chapter to read first.

Is this medical advice?

No. The guide is general, evidence-informed education. It does not replace individual assessment or advice from professionals who know your child.
